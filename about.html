About

If there's one character I relate to, it's Scrat from Ice Age: always chasing the next acorn, adapting to whatever comes my way, and finding a way forward regardless of what the world throws at me.

Adaptability has been a constant theme in my life. Growing up, I changed schools seven times, often in the middle of the academic year. Each transition taught me to embrace the unfamiliar, build relationships quickly, and find my footing in new environments.

I started my academic journey in Mechanical Engineering at Thapar University, driven by my love for physics and problem-solving. However, a defining personal experience, my father's cancer diagnosis in 2016, made healthcare deeply personal and pushed me to be more intentional about the impact I wanted to create. This eventually led me to pivot into healthcare consulting.

Over the past five years at ZS Associates, I've worked on commercialization strategy and market research for global pharmaceutical companies, while increasingly exploring how AI can transform the healthcare industry. From developing AI-powered research solutions to contributing to large-scale enterprise AI transformation initiatives, I've enjoyed working at the intersection of strategy, technology, and execution.

Today, I'm pursuing my MBA at UNC Kenan-Flagler to strengthen my business foundation, broaden my perspective, and prepare myself to tackle bigger, more complex challenges.

Beyond the work itself, I believe in bringing positive energy to teams, creating an environment where people enjoy working together, and taking ownership of delivering meaningful outcomes.

I'm still chasing the next acorn, but the goal is bigger than the acorn itself: to keep learning, embrace change, bring people along, and make a meaningful difference along the way.
